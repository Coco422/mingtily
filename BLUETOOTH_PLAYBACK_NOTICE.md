# Bluetooth Playback Notice

Mingtily may show a warning when meeting audio is played through a Bluetooth output device. Bluetooth devices can switch profiles or sample rates while microphones are active, which may reduce playback quality or change how audio sounds.

## What to try

1. Play the same recording through the Mac's built-in speakers or wired headphones.
2. Disconnect and reconnect the Bluetooth device, then confirm that it is using its high-quality media profile rather than a call/headset profile.
3. Close applications that are actively using the Bluetooth microphone.
4. Open the saved recording in another local player to distinguish a playback-device issue from a damaged recording.

If the recording sounds wrong on every output device and player, include the operating-system version, output device, recording format, and reproduction steps in a GitHub issue. Do not attach private meeting audio unless you have removed sensitive content and explicitly intend to share it.

This notice is troubleshooting guidance, not a claim that every Bluetooth playback issue originates outside Mingtily.
